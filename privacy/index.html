Bowl & Roll Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how Bowl & Roll (“the app”) handles information. The app is provided by Scott Richardson, trading as HikaruTech, in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at team@hikarutech.com.

Summary

Bowl & Roll is a local multiplayer bowling game. It does not require an account, does not show advertising, does not include in-app purchases, and does not use analytics or other third-party software development kits. Information you create in the app stays on your device, except for a small amount of data sent only to another phone on the same Wi-Fi network if you choose to play a Live Game.

Information stored on your device

The app may store the following on your device only:

  • Match records and game history
  • App settings
  • Bowl kit customisation
  • An optional profile name
  • An optional profile photo and bowl photos you choose from your device gallery

This information is not uploaded to us or to any server.

Live Game on the same Wi-Fi network

If you start or join a Live Game, the app uses a local network connection to play with another phone on the same Wi-Fi network. The two phones exchange the player’s display name, a room code, and the gameplay moves needed to play the match. There is no game server operated by us. Photos are not sent. We do not receive this information.

Device permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Internet access, so a Live Game can connect to another phone on the same local network
  • Vibration, for optional haptic feedback during play
  • Access to photos or media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or, on older Android versions, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), only if you choose a profile photo or bowl photo from your gallery

You can refuse or later revoke photo access in your device settings. The app will still work without it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data with third parties for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no third-party software development kits.

Legal basis and UK GDPR

If UK GDPR applies, we process any personal data that may be stored on your device (such as an optional profile name or photo you choose) so that we can provide the app’s features to you, which is the performance of a contract, or because you have chosen to use those optional features. Because this information stays on your device and is not sent to us, we do not hold a copy of it.

You may still have rights in relation to personal data under UK law, including the right to be informed, to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. Because we do not hold a copy of your information on our systems, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete from a server. If you believe we do hold any personal data about you, or you want to exercise a privacy right, email team@hikarutech.com.

How to delete your data

To delete information stored by the app, uninstall Bowl & Roll or clear the app’s data in your device settings. After you do that, the stored match records, settings, customisation, and any optional profile name or photos chosen in the app are removed from the device.

Children

Bowl & Roll is not directed at children, but it is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because the app does not collect information from players, a child can use it without creating an account or sending personal data to us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, email team@hikarutech.com.
